# Fixed deposit (FD)

**What it is:** You lend a bank a lump sum for a fixed time (from days to years) at a rate agreed on day one.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Risk** | Very low. Bank FDs are insured up to ₹5 lakh per person per bank (principal and interest together). |
| **Lock-in** | The chosen term. Breaking early usually means a penalty and a lower rate. |
| **Liquidity** | Medium. You can break it, at a cost. |
| **Return** | Fixed at the start. Usually higher than a savings account. |
| **Tax** | Interest is added to your income and taxed at your slab. Banks may deduct tax at source above a limit. |

**Good for:** Money you know you won't need for a set period.
**Watch out for:** Locking money you might need, and returns that may not beat inflation after tax.

*Rates and tax rules change, so no figures are quoted here. Last reviewed: November 2026. Check current ones at [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in) and your bank's own rate page.*
