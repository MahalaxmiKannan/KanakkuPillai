# Savings account

**What it is:** Money in a bank that you can take out whenever you want, and that earns a small amount of interest.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Risk** | Very low. Deposits in most banks are insured up to ₹5 lakh per person per bank. |
| **Lock-in** | None. |
| **Liquidity** | Instant (UPI, ATM, transfer). |
| **Return** | Low and variable; the bank sets it and can change it. It often trails inflation. |
| **Tax** | Interest counts as your income. Small amounts have a deduction; check the current limit. |

**Good for:** Everyday money and a first stop for your emergency fund.
**Watch out for:** Minimum-balance charges, and the fact that low interest can lose value against rising prices.

*Rates and tax rules change, so no figures are quoted here. Last reviewed: November 2026. Check the current ones at [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in) and [Income Tax Department](https://www.incometax.gov.in).*
