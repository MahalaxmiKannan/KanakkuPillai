# National Pension System (NPS)

**What it is:** A government-backed retirement scheme run under PFRDA. You pay in during your working life and get money after retirement.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Risk** | Low to high, depending on how much goes to shares versus bonds. You choose within limits. |
| **Lock-in** | Mostly until about age 60, with limited early withdrawals. Part of the final amount must buy an annuity (a regular pension). |
| **Liquidity** | Low. |
| **Return** | Not fixed; depends on the funds and markets. |
| **Tax** | Deposits may qualify for deductions, and withdrawals have their own rules. These change often, so check the current ones. |

**Good for:** Long-term retirement saving by people who can leave the money alone for decades.
**Watch out for:** The lock-in and the annuity requirement. It isn't suitable for goals within a few years.

*Rates and tax rules change, so no figures are quoted here. Last reviewed: November 2026. Check current ones at [PFRDA](https://www.pfrda.org.in) and [Income Tax Department](https://www.incometax.gov.in).*
