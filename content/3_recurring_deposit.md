# Recurring deposit (RD)

**What it is:** Like an FD, but you put in a fixed amount every month for a set period, and earn a fixed rate.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Risk** | Very low (bank RDs are insured like other bank deposits; Post Office RDs are backed by the government). |
| **Lock-in** | The chosen term. Closing early usually means a lower rate. |
| **Liquidity** | Low to medium. |
| **Return** | Fixed when you open it. |
| **Tax** | Interest is added to your income and taxed at your slab. |

**Good for:** Building a saving habit when you don't have a lump sum.
**Watch out for:** Late or missed instalments can bring penalties.

*Rates and tax rules change, so no figures are quoted here. Last reviewed: November 2026. Check current ones at [India Post](https://www.indiapost.gov.in) and [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in).*
