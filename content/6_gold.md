# Gold

**What it is:** Gold held as jewellery or coins, or through paper and digital forms such as gold ETFs and gold funds.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Risk** | Medium to high. The price moves up and down and can stay flat for years. |
| **Lock-in** | None for physical gold, gold ETFs or funds. |
| **Liquidity** | Good, but physical gold sells below its buying price (making charges, purity checks). |
| **Return** | Not fixed. It earns no interest. |
| **Tax** | Gains are taxed when you sell; rates depend on the form and how long you held. Check the current rules. |

**Good for:** Some people use it to spread risk, since it doesn't always move with shares.
**Watch out for:** Making charges and storage on physical gold, and "digital gold" apps, which may sit outside the regulators' protections. Check who regulates what you are buying.

*Rates and tax rules change, so no figures are quoted here. Last reviewed: November 2026. Check current ones at [SEBI Investor](https://investor.sebi.gov.in) and [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in).*
