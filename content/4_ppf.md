# Public Provident Fund (PPF)

**What it is:** A government savings scheme for long-term saving, available at banks and post offices.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Risk** | Very low. The government backs it. |
| **Lock-in** | 15 years, with limited partial withdrawals after the early years and loans against the balance in the first few years. |
| **Liquidity** | Low. |
| **Return** | The rate is set by the government and reviewed every quarter, so it can change. |
| **Tax** | Interest is currently tax-free, and deposits may qualify for a deduction under the old tax regime. Check the current rules. |

**Good for:** Very long-term, low-risk saving.
**Watch out for:** The long lock-in and the yearly deposit cap. Don't put in money you may need soon.

*Rates and tax rules change, so no figures are quoted here. Last reviewed: November 2026. Check current ones at [India Post](https://www.indiapost.gov.in) and [National Savings Institute](https://www.nsiindia.gov.in).*
